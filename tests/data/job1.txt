Backend Platform Engineer

Company: Northbound Cloud Services
Location: Toronto, ON (Hybrid – 2 days/week in office)
Compensation: CAD $120,000 – $150,000 base + bonus + equity
Employment Type: Full-time

Who We Are

Northbound Cloud Services builds scalable infrastructure tools used by mid-sized SaaS companies across North America. Our platform helps teams deploy, monitor, and operate distributed systems without needing a dedicated SRE team. We’re engineers at heart, and we care deeply about reliability, simplicity, and clean system design.

Job Summary

We’re looking for a Back-end Platform Engineer to help design and evolve the core services that power our cloud platform. You’ll work on high-throughput APIs, distributed systems, and internal tooling used by both customers and fellow engineers.

Responsibilities

Design and implement backend services in a microservices environment

Build and maintain REST and gRPC APIs with a focus on performance and reliability

Work with distributed data stores and caching layers

Improve system observability, monitoring, and alerting

Collaborate with front-end, infrastructure, and product teams

Participate in architecture reviews and technical decision-making

Contribute to on-call rotation and incident response

Required Skills & Experience

3+ years of professional back-end software development experience

Strong proficiency in at least one backend language (Go, Java, Python, or C++)

Experience with SQL databases and data modeling

Familiarity with distributed systems concepts

Experience with Docker and containerized deployments

Solid understanding of networking fundamentals

Nice to Have

Experience with Kubernetes or cloud-native architectures

Exposure to event-driven systems or message queues

Background in performance optimization or systems programming

Benefits

Competitive salary and annual performance bonus

Stock options

Extended health, dental, and vision coverage

Flexible work hours and hybrid work model

Learning budget for courses, conferences, and books
