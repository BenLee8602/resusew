Embedded Software Engineer (IoT Systems)

Company: Aurora Embedded Technologies
Location: Kitchener, ON (On-site)
Compensation: CAD $95,000 – $125,000
Employment Type: Full-time

Who We Are

Aurora Embedded Technologies designs and manufactures connected hardware for environmental monitoring, smart infrastructure, and industrial automation. Our devices operate in the real world — harsh conditions, limited power, and strict reliability requirements. We build systems that engineers trust when failure is not an option.

Job Summary

We’re seeking an Embedded Software Engineer to work on firmware and low-level systems for our next generation of IoT devices. You’ll work close to the hardware and collaborate with electrical, mechanical, and cloud teams to deliver robust end-to-end solutions.

Responsibilities

Develop firmware in C/C++ for microcontrollers and embedded Linux systems

Interface with sensors, communication modules, and peripherals

Implement communication protocols (MQTT, SPI, I2C, UART, CAN)

Optimize code for memory usage, performance, and power efficiency

Debug hardware/software issues using oscilloscopes, logic analyzers, and JTAG

Contribute to system architecture and hardware bring-up

Write technical documentation and test procedures

Required Skills & Experience

2+ years of embedded software development experience

Strong proficiency in C or C++

Experience working with microcontrollers (ARM Cortex-M, ESP32, etc.)

Familiarity with real-time or resource-constrained environments

Understanding of low-level debugging and hardware interaction

Nice to Have

Experience with embedded Linux or Yocto

Knowledge of wireless protocols (BLE, LoRa, Wi-Fi)

Exposure to OTA updates and secure bootloaders

Competitive salary

Health and dental benefits

RRSP matching program

Relocation assistance (if applicable)

Hands-on access to labs and hardware

Tight-knit engineering team with real ownership
